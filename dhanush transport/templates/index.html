How Dhanush Dual-Transit Works

Solving empty mileage and accelerating transit speeds by unifying passenger mobility and freight logistics into a cohesive ecosystem.

1

Dual Capacity Reservation

Passengers book comfortable seats online, while businesses and senders register parcels. Our engine calculates passenger seat allocation alongside unused lower luggage bay capacity.

2

Smart Cargo-Hitching

Express parcels (< 150 kg) are loaded into the climate-controlled cargo decks of passenger coaches. They move at passenger bus speeds, achieving same-day delivery without waiting for slow freight consolidation.

3

Unified Telematics & Hub Delivery

Both travelers and freight receivers track the exact same vehicle using PNR or AWB tracking codes. Upon arrival at the terminal, passengers disembark while parcels are instantly sorted for last-mile delivery.
